Reflectis / Virtuademy Platform

2021 - Ongoing

An enterprise-grade Extended Reality (XR) and WebGL platform enabling scalable, synchronous multiplayer training and educational metaverses.

TEAM: Anothereality | ROLE: Lead Technical Designer & Project Manager

  • Spearheaded the design and ideation of core features from the platform's inception. Successfully tackled the architectural challenge of creating a seamless cross-play experience connecting WebGL and VR.
  • Architected the platform's foundational UX/UI structure from the ground up, including mapping comprehensive login flows and conceptualizing the "Tablet" interactive menu.
  • Co-designed comprehensive backoffice functionalities integrating systems for direct 3D environment uploads via Unity, Addressables management through Azure, and dynamic asset spawning.

2025

A narrative-driven 3D first-person adventure edugame designed to train the corporate workforce in foundational cybersecurity principles.

TEAM: Anothereality | ROLE: Lead Technical Designer & Project Manager

  • Designed the complete experience flow and 12 distinct scenarios tackling various cybersecurity threats, collaborating closely with a Subject Matter Expert (SME).
